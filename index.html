Our story

A short life. A lasting impact.

A precious baby boy was born 14 weeks early.

His mother went into preterm labor and was rushed by ambulance to Froedtert & the Medical College of Wisconsin and Children's Hospital in Milwaukee. A day later, he passed away.

From the depths of grief in the hours, days, and weeks that followed, the Dawoodbhai Legacy Project and Fund was created. We honor his memory by making sure other families feel supported during the most difficult time of their lives.

How we help

When the unthinkable happens, money should be the last thing on your mind.

Loss brings sudden costs no parent plans for: a casket, transportation, a burial plot, a headstone. We help carry that weight so parents can grieve.

So far, we have supported 0 families and gifted more than 75 hats, blankets, and memory boxes.
