Partner programs

Organizations we trust

No single organization can meet every need after a loss. These partners offer comfort, remembrance, and community alongside our financial support.

Molly Bears

Comfort

Weighted teddy bears made for families coping with any form of infant loss. Each bear can be made to the baby's weight, giving parents something to hold.

mollybears.org →

Now I Lay Me Down to Sleep

Remembrance

Free remembrance portraits for parents who have experienced the death of a baby, taken by volunteer photographers with gentleness and care.

nowilaymedowntosleep.org →

Chasing the Rainbows

Community

Daily support services for survivors of infertility, miscarriage, stillbirth, and infant loss.

chasingtherainbows.org →

Star Legacy Foundation

Research and advocacy

A community of families, health professionals, researchers, and policy makers working to reduce pregnancy loss and neonatal death and to improve care for families.

starlegacyfoundation.org →

Partner with us

Help us reach families sooner.

Most families find us through someone who was there when it happened. If you work with grieving parents, we would love to work with you.

Hospitals and NICUs

Social workers, nurses, and bereavement coordinators can refer families to us directly or reach out on a family's behalf.

Funeral homes and counselors

Share our support with families planning a service for their baby or working through grief.

Companies and communities
